## How to rerun

```bash
RAW_DATA_DIR=~/Projects/reached-hq/raw-data python analysis/profiling/profile_psa_school_age_population.py
```

Both workbooks must be at `$RAW_DATA_DIR/psa/original/`. The cross-checks are optional: X-1 runs if the [psa_population_per_age_group](../psa_population_per_age_group/README.md#files) CSV is there with the SHA-256 on its card, X-2 if the PSGC 2Q 2026 workbook is there with the SHA-256 on the [psa_psgc card](../psa_psgc/README.md). Otherwise each prints `skipped`. Each finding below prints under its ID. The column statistics in [data_dictionary.md](data_dictionary.md) come from `analysis/profiling/dictionary_psa_school_age_population.py`. Two runs on the same files give identical output.

## Summary

Usable as the school-age population for cities and municipalities in 2020 and 2024: every count is complete, sexes add up, and every region and province equals the sum of its places, so nothing is missing (O-2 to O-4). Ages 5-17, both sexes, total population: 28,680,299 in 2020 and 27,569,038 in 2024 (O-8). It has **no codes**: places are names only, and their level comes from the sums (O-2). Matching the 2024 names to PSGC 2Q 2026 within the province places 1,602 of 1,642 one-to-one; the other 40 are naming differences (Western Samar, the Special Geographic Area, 6 renamed or respelled LGUs) that a short reviewed list resolves (X-2). The 2020 names also need the 2022-2024 boundary changes (O-7). Total and household population are separate sheets and must not be mixed (O-5). It holds census years only; a 2023 value would be the team's estimate.

## Changes across files or years

- Layout: 2020 wide (sex as column bands), 2024 long (sex as rows) (O-1).
- Geography: 17 to 18 regions (Negros Island Region), 82 to 83 provinces (Maguindanao split, Samar renamed Western Samar, Interim Province renamed Special Geographic Area), the same 1,642 cities and municipalities in count but not in membership (O-7).
- Labels: old names in brackets in 2020, mostly gone in 2024; footnote markers only in 2024; region label styles differ (O-6, O-7).
- Titles: the 2024 household sheet title names only "City/Municipality", though it has the same levels as the total sheet (O-1, O-5).
